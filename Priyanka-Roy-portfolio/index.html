Case Study : SYS.01

Venture Soul Business Portfolio

Architected a premium corporate identity and web experience for Venture Soul. Focused heavily on establishing trust, seamless content navigation, and translating complex business narratives into highly engaging visual designs.

Role UI/UX Designer
Tools Figma
Client Venture Soul Partners
Type Corporate Portfolio

01 // PROJECT OVERVIEW & DESIGN GOALS

Venture Soul required a sophisticated digital portfolio to firmly establish their corporate identity in a competitive market. As the lead designer, my goal was to craft a premium user experience that seamlessly blended their brand storytelling with accessible industry insights. The primary challenge was structuring complex corporate timelines and thought-leadership articles into an intuitive, visually elegant interface that conveyed authority and trust.

venturesoul.in — Design Prototypes

02 // INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & USER FLOW

Designed a flattened, intuitive site map ensuring investors and partners could locate critical information with minimal friction:

  • Corporate Identity (About Us): Strategically designed layouts prioritizing whitespace and professional imagery to highlight leadership credibility and core values.
  • The Journey: Conceived a highly interactive, vertical timeline UI to visually guide users through the company's historical milestones without overwhelming text.
  • Insights & Media: Developed an editorial-style grid system for analytical articles and press mentions, ensuring a consistent visual rhythm.
  • Blog Ecosystem: Engineered an accessible blog interface featuring dynamic category tags, search functionality, and optimized typography for long-form reading.

03 // DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETICS

Typography & Scale Established a strict typographic hierarchy using modern sans-serif fonts to ensure readability across all devices.
Brand Integration Translated the corporate style guide into a sleek digital color palette, utilizing subtle gradients and high-contrast elements.
Component Library Built a comprehensive Figma component library (buttons, cards, navbars) to streamline development handoff.

04 // THE IMPACT

Delivered a polished, responsive, and deeply engaging user interface that perfectly encapsulated Venture Soul's market positioning. The resulting design provided the client with an elegant digital foundation to showcase their authority, resulting in heightened brand consistency and incredibly positive feedback during stakeholder reviews.

Case Study : SYS.02

Pricexa Price Monitoring Platform

Role UI Design
Tools Figma
Client Internal
Focus SaaS Dashboards

01 // THE PROBLEM

E-commerce retailers face constant margin pressures but lacked an intuitive way to track and analyze competitor pricing in real-time. Existing solutions relied heavily on dense, spreadsheet-like interfaces, causing cognitive overload and making it nearly impossible for users to quickly identify market trends, price drops, or actionable pricing strategies.

02 // THE DESIGN PROCESS

Modeled heavily after industry-leading platforms like Pricefy, I focused on Information Architecture and data visualization to reduce friction and highlight key retail metrics:

  • Competitor Tracking Dashboards: Redesigned the main interface to prioritize high-level metrics (price drops, competitor stock status, smart pricing suggestions) visually over raw data tables.
  • Data Visualization: Prototyped interactive charts and graphs to make historical price trend analysis intuitive and accessible at a glance.
  • Dynamic Alert Workflows: Streamlined the user flow for configuring complex, rule-based price tracking alerts, reducing cognitive load through step-by-step modal interactions.
  • Product Matching UI: Built a specialized, drag-and-drop workflow interface allowing users to manually map and verify identical products across different competitor URLs.

03 // THE OUTCOME

The highly visual, streamlined UI drastically reduced the time it took for businesses to analyze market data. The intuitive chart interactions and clear data hierarchy transformed overwhelming scraping data into actionable business intelligence, resulting in a significantly smoother onboarding experience for new retail clients.

Case Study : SYS.03

Pharmaceutra E-Commerce

Role UI
Tools Figma
Client Pharmaceutra UK
Focus Healthcare E-Commerce

01 // THE PROBLEM

Pharmaceutra is a UK-based online pharmacy platform requiring users to complete medical questionnaires before purchasing prescription treatments (e.g., Weight Loss, Dermatology, ED). The primary challenge was that complex, clinical forms often caused high cart abandonment rates. The client needed a platform that felt highly secure and professional, while making the consultation and checkout process completely frictionless for the end-user.

02 // THE DESIGN PROCESS

My design approach balanced e-commerce conversion optimization with stringent healthcare trust signals:

  • Product Discovery & IA: Designed clear, card-based category navigation (e.g., Acne, Asthma, Hair Loss) allowing users to easily self-identify their condition and browse suitable treatments rather than searching through medical jargon.
  • Consultation Workflow: Broke down lengthy medical questionnaires into bite-sized, progress-tracked steps. This drastically reduced cognitive load and guided the user gently through the virtual consultation phase.
  • Trust & Credibility: Established a clean, clinical color palette (whites, deep blues, and greens). Strategically placed GPhC pharmacy registration badges, prescriber review snippets, and secure payment icons at critical friction points in the checkout journey to reassure patients.

03 // THE OUTCOME

The resulting design was a highly accessible, mobile-responsive e-commerce experience. By streamlining the medical questionnaire into an intuitive step-by-step UI and elevating the brand's visual credibility, the platform successfully fostered patient trust and smoothed the pathway from condition discovery directly to secure prescription checkout.
